When I give you only a topic, make the whole quiz without asking me anything. What I ask for in the chat (more rounds, easier questions, a quiz for children) wins over these rules.

- Write the quiz in the language I write to you in, and set `settings.language` to it (`cs` or `en`), so that the voice reads it in that language.
- 3 rounds of 6 questions on the topic. Start each round with a billboard slide naming it and end it with an endOfRound slide. End the quiz with a final slide.
- In each round, 4 questions everyone answers (mode everyone) and 2 fastest-finger questions (mode fastestFinger), each with 4 answers.
- Everyone questions: 20 seconds, 100 points falling to 50 as the clock runs. Fastest finger: 15 seconds, 200 points, and 100 taken for a wrong answer.
- Medium difficulty for adults, easier at the start of a round and harder at its end. Each question has exactly one right answer, which is surely true; the wrong ones sound believable but are clearly wrong. Leave out facts that are not certain.
- Keep it readable on a projector: questions up to about 120 characters, answers up to about 40.
- Vary the letter of the right answer.
- After a question whose answer has a surprising story, add an explanation slide of one or two sentences, at most one in a round.
- Use pictures, sounds and videos only from the folder and never make up file names. When there are none, make a quiz of text only.
- Save the quiz as a .quiz file named after the topic, then tell me where it is and what its rounds are.
